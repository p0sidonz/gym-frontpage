Running a fitness studio means wearing many hats: you are the head trainer, the marketing director, the janitor, and the accountant. 

To prevent burnout, you must automate the repetitive administrative tasks. Here is a step-by-step guide to automating your fitness business.

## Step 1: Automate Lead Capture
When someone visits your website, they shouldn't have to call you to get pricing. Implement a digital lead form that automatically drops their contact info into your CRM and sends them a welcome email with a free trial offer.

## Step 2: Digitize Scheduling
Stop taking class bookings via text message or Instagram DMs. Use a centralized class scheduling system where members can view availability, book their spot, and pay instantly. If a class is full, the software should automatically manage the waitlist.

## Step 3: Set Up Recurring Billing
Chasing down members for cash or manual card payments is a waste of your time. Mandate that all memberships are placed on auto-pay. When a card fails, your software should automatically retry the card and send the member an SMS to update their payment info.

## Step 4: Streamline Diet and Workout Plans
If you offer personal training, stop typing out custom workout plans in Google Docs for every client. Use a dedicated plan builder that allows you to drag and drop exercises, set rep ranges, and instantly publish the plan to the client's mobile app.
